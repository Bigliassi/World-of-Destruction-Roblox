# World of Destruction (Roblox) — Version 1.0 Game Guide & Instructions

Welcome to **World of Destruction v1.0** for Roblox! Set in the hand-crafted open world of **Leo City**, you take the wheel of high-powered demolition vehicles, chain massive destruction combos up to **10x OVERDRIVE**, launch off stunt mega-ramps, upgrade your garage at the **Speed Shop**, climb 50 player progression levels, and survive 5-Star Police Heat.

## 3. Core Gameplay Loop

1. **Summon & Drive (`[V]`):** Hop into your starter **Apex GT** or **Cruiser V8** at Leo Plaza. Follow the glowing 3D onboarding waypoint beam if it is your first session.
2. **Smash & Chain Combos (`4.5s Window`):** Every cone, crate, hydrant, fence, streetlight, explosive barrel, parked car, or building chunk you smash awards **Cash ($)**, **Chaos Score**, and **XP**, while refreshing your **4.5-second Combo Timer**.
   - **Combo Multiplier Tiers:**
     - `1x` (0–1 hits)
     - `2x COMBO!` (2–4 hits)
     - `3x DESTRUCTION!` (5–7 hits)
     - `5x RAMPAGE!` (8–12 hits)
     - `8x MAYHEM!` (13–19 hits)
     - `10x OVERDRIVE!` (20+ chain!)
   - **Style Callouts:** Earn bonus multipliers for `HYDRO BLAST!` (fire hydrants), `CHAIN EXPLOSION!` (barrels/fuel tanks), `MEGA AIR!` (stunt jumps), `DRIFT KING!` (corner slides), `NEAR MISS!`, and `COP TAKEDOWN!`.
   - **Combo Completion Bank Bonus:** When your combo timer expires cleanly on a chain of 3+, you bank an extra **+25% Cash Bonus** on everything earned during that combo!
3. **Complete Live Chaos Missions:** Timed rotating objectives appear at the top of your HUD (e.g., *"60s Destruction Derby: Cause $5,000 damage!"*, *"Smash 10 Fire Hydrants!"*, *"Survive 3-Star Heat for 45s!"*, *"Wreck 3 Police Cruisers!"*). Completing a mission grants a huge Cash, Chaos, and **+250 XP** payout.
4. **Survive Police Heat (1★ to 5★):** Causing mayhem builds Wanted Heat. Higher Heat increases your **Wanted Bounty Cash Multiplier** up to **3.0x** at 5 Stars, but brings aggressive Patrol Cruisers, Interceptors, and SWAT Units! Ram cruisers at high speed to wreck them for `COP TAKEDOWN!` bonuses, or outrun them to cool down your Heat.
5. **Upgrade at the Speed Shop (`[B]`):** Drive onto the glowing cyan Speed Shop pads at `(120, 120)` or `(-120, -120)`—or press `[B]` anywhere—to upgrade your 5 core performance tracks and unlock heavy demolition vehicles.

## 6. How to Publish to Roblox & Enable Live Cloud DataStores

1. Open `Roblox/WorldOfDestruction.rbxlx` in Roblox Studio.
2. Click **File → Publish to Roblox As...** and create a new experience titled **World of Destruction**.
3. Open **File → Game Settings → Security**:
   - Toggle **Enable Studio Access to API Services** to **ON** (enables live cloud `DataStoreService` in Studio; note that even if left OFF, `PlayerDataService` automatically falls back to an in-memory store so Studio playtesting never errors).
   - Toggle **Allow Third Party Sales** if needed for group assets.
4. Open **File → Game Settings → Monetization** to create your live GamePasses and Developer Products (see `Roblox/docs/MONETIZATION-PLAN.md` for exact IDs and setup).
5. Click **File → Publish to Roblox** whenever you ship an update!
